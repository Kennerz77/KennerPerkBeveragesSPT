# Dr K's Perk Beverages

The perk machines and perk bottles for **Dr K's SPZombies**: Jugger-Nog, Speed Cola, Double Tap Root Beer, Deadshot Daiquiri, Stamin-Up, Quick Revive, Mule Kick, Widow's Wine and PHD Flopper. Each machine hums, lights up with the power, plays its jingle, and sells its perk with the full drink in first person.

Version 0.1.0, for **SPT 4.1.6**. Made by **Kenner**.

> **On its own this mod adds nothing to your game yet.** It's the perk side of Dr K's SPZombies: the machines stand on the zombies maps and only work there. Nothing appears in your hideout, at traders, in loot or on the flea market, and no perk bottle ever reaches your normal character.

## Requirements

| Mod | Version | Why |
|---|---|---|
| SPT | 4.1.x (made on 4.1.6) | |

Used by [Dr K's SPZombies](https://github.com/Kennerz77/KennerZombiesSPT), which needs this mod.

## Installing

1. Close the game and the SPT server.
2. Extract the zip into your SPT folder (the one with the `SPT_Runtime` folder). You should end up with:
   - `BepInEx\plugins\PerkBeverages\`
   - `SPT_Runtime\user\mods\PerkBeverages\`
3. Start the server, then the game.

## Settings (F12)

- **Volume:** Jingle, Sting, Hum, Power-on, Sparks (no power), Purchase and Drinking.
- **HUD:** where your perk icons sit on screen, and their size.

## Compatibility

- Never changes another mod's files, settings or load order.
- Works with Fika co-op as it is; Fika itself isn't changed.

## Uninstalling

Delete `BepInEx\plugins\PerkBeverages\` and `SPT_Runtime\user\mods\PerkBeverages\`. Every server start clears any perk bottle from all profiles, so nothing is left behind.
